Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Jane 370Z is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not available to anyone else.

Google user data

  • What is accessed: Jane can search and read files in the owner's Google Drive (read-only) and can create and manage only the files she creates herself, which are used for backups of her own configuration.
  • How it is used: Drive content is read only when the owner asks a question that needs it, to give the owner an answer. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.
  • Storage: Sign-in credentials are stored only on the owner's own device. Drive content is not stored beyond what is needed to answer a request.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not sold or shared with anyone. To answer a question, excerpts may be processed by the AI voice service that powers Jane's conversation, solely to produce that answer.
  • Limited use: Jane's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: The owner can remove Jane's access at any time from Google Account permissions.
